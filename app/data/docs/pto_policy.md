# Paid Time Off Policy (POL-HR-003)

This policy describes paid time off (PTO) for full-time office-based employees of Meridian Basin Energy Services. Field rotation employees are covered in section 5.

## 1. Accrual
PTO accrues each pay period based on years of continuous service:
- 0 to 4 years of service: 15 days per year.
- 5 to 9 years of service: 20 days per year.
- 10 or more years of service: 25 days per year.
Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to scheduled hours.

## 2. Carryover
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1 unless the HR Director approves an exception for business reasons.

## 3. Requesting time off
PTO requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted in the HR system at least two weeks in advance. Shorter requests should be submitted as early as practical. Managers should respond to PTO requests within 3 business days.

## 4. Holidays
Meridian observes 9 paid company holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published each December.

## 5. Field rotation employees
Field employees on a 14/14 rotation (14 days on, 14 days off) do not accrue standard PTO. Instead they receive 4 additional paid days per year that may be used to swap rotation dates with supervisor approval.

## 6. Payout
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on termination where required by state law.
