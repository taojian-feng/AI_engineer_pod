# Inventory Management Standard (STD-SCM-007)

This standard defines how Meridian Basin Energy Services manages spare parts and consumables at its district warehouses.

## 1. Reorder point
Each stocked item has a reorder point (ROP). The ROP is calculated as average daily usage multiplied by the supplier lead time in days, plus safety stock. When on-hand quantity plus quantity on open POs falls below the ROP, a replenishment PO should be raised.

## 2. Order quantity
The standard replenishment quantity is the amount needed to bring on-hand plus on-order quantity back up to the target stock level, which is twice the reorder point, rounded up to the supplier's minimum order quantity.

## 3. Critical spares
Items flagged as critical spares (for example PDC drill bits, BOP seal kits and high-pressure pump fluid ends) must hold safety stock equal to at least 14 days of average usage. A stock-out of a critical spare must be reported to the District Manager within 24 hours.

## 4. Cycle counting
Items are classified by annual spend. Class A items are cycle counted monthly, class B items quarterly and class C items twice a year. A count variance above 2% triggers a recount and a root-cause review.

## 5. Quarantine
Received goods that fail incoming inspection are moved to the quarantine location and may not be issued to jobs. Quarantined stock does not count towards on-hand quantity for reorder calculations.

## 6. Obsolete stock
Items with no usage for 24 months are reviewed for disposal by the Inventory Control team.
