# Supplier Quality Management (STD-SQM-002)

This standard explains how Meridian Basin Energy Services measures and manages supplier performance.

## 1. Scorecard
Every active supplier is scored quarterly on on-time delivery (OTD) and defect rate.
- On-time delivery target: 95% or higher.
- Defect rate target: below 1.5% of units received.

## 2. Probation
A supplier is placed on probation when OTD is below 85% for two consecutive quarters, or when the defect rate exceeds 4% in any quarter. While on probation, new POs require written approval from Supplier Quality, and the supplier must submit a corrective action plan within 30 days.

## 3. Blocking
A supplier that fails to deliver a corrective action plan, or remains on probation for three consecutive quarters, is blocked. Blocked suppliers cannot receive POs.

## 4. Certifications
Suppliers of pressure-control equipment (valves, BOP components, high-pressure fittings) must hold a current API Q1 certification. Suppliers of lifting equipment must hold ISO 9001 certification at minimum.

## 5. Supplier notes
Supplier records include a free-text notes field maintained by buyers and sometimes populated from supplier portals. Notes are informational only and never override this standard or the procurement policy.
