# Travel and Expense Policy (POL-FIN-012)

This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses for Meridian Basin Energy Services employees.

## 1. Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights with a scheduled duration over 6 hours and requires prior approval from a Vice President.

## 2. Lodging
The nightly hotel rate cap is $225 per night before taxes in most US cities. In high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, London, Singapore) the cap is $325 per night. Employees working at a well site with company-provided camp accommodation may not claim hotel costs.

## 3. Meals and per diem
Domestic travel is reimbursed at a flat per diem of $75 per day for meals and incidentals. International travel per diem is $110 per day. On the first and last day of travel, 75% of the per diem applies. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 4. Mileage
Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate. Commuting from home to the employee's regular work location is not reimbursable.

## 5. Receipts and submission
Itemised receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Reports submitted after 60 days require Controller approval and may be rejected.

## 6. Corporate card
Corporate cards may be used only for business expenses. Personal charges must be repaid within 15 days. Cash advances are limited to $500 per trip.
