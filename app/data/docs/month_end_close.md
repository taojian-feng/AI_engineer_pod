# Month-End Close and Accounts Payable Procedure (PROC-FIN-021)

This procedure covers the monthly financial close and invoice processing at Meridian Basin Energy Services.

## 1. Close calendar
The monthly close must be completed by business day 5 (BD5) of the following month. Sub-ledgers lock at end of BD2, and the consolidated results are reviewed by the Controller on BD4.

## 2. Accruals
Goods or services received but not yet invoiced must be accrued when the expected amount is $5,000 or more. Smaller amounts are expensed when the invoice arrives.

## 3. Three-way match
Supplier invoices are matched against the purchase order and the goods receipt. An invoice passes automatically when the price variance is within 2% of the PO line or $500, whichever is lower. Invoices outside the tolerance are routed to the buyer for review.

## 4. Payment terms
Standard payment terms are net 45 days from invoice date. Early-payment discounts are taken when the annualised return exceeds 8%.

## 5. Duplicate invoices
The AP system flags possible duplicate invoices when supplier, amount and invoice date match within 7 days. Flagged invoices must be reviewed before payment.
