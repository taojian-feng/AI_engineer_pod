# Procurement Policy (POL-PRC-004)

Meridian Basin Energy Services ("Meridian") is a fictional oilfield services company used for interview practice. This policy governs how all purchase orders (POs) are raised, approved and issued.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to every purchase of goods and services made by Meridian employees, contractors and automated systems, including AI agents acting on behalf of an employee. Automated systems must follow the same approval limits as a human buyer.

## 2. Approval thresholds
Approval limits are based on the total PO value in US dollars, including freight.
- Up to $10,000: the requesting buyer may issue the PO directly.
- $10,000.01 to $50,000: approval from the Category Manager is required before the PO is issued.
- Above $50,000: approval from the Director of Supply Chain is required, and at least three competitive quotes must be attached to the PO.
Splitting a requirement into several smaller POs to stay under a threshold is prohibited and is treated as a policy violation.

## 3. Preferred and approved suppliers
Buyers must use a supplier from the Approved Supplier List (ASL). Where a preferred supplier exists for an item, it should be used unless the buyer documents a reason. Suppliers on probation may not receive new POs without written approval from Supplier Quality. Suppliers with status "blocked" may never receive POs.

## 4. Duplicate orders
Before raising a PO, the buyer must check for open POs for the same item. If open POs already cover the shortfall, no new PO should be raised. Expediting the existing PO is preferred.

## 5. Emergency purchases
In a well-site emergency that threatens safety or causes non-productive time, an Operations Manager may authorise an emergency purchase of up to $25,000 by phone. The PO must be raised in the system within 2 business days of the emergency purchase.

## 6. Sole-source purchases
A sole-source purchase (no competitive quotes) above $10,000 requires a written sole-source justification approved by the Category Manager. The justification must explain why no alternative supplier can meet the technical requirement.

## 7. Records
PO records, quotes and approvals are retained for seven years.
